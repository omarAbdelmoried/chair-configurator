# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Online shoppers comparing chair options while deciding what to buy.

## Product Purpose

Help shoppers explore chair configurations, see each change in a live 3D preview, and understand the price before requesting a product.

## Brand Personality

Clear, calm, and practical. The interface should keep attention on the chair and make choices feel easy to compare.

## Anti-references

Avoid cluttered controls, hidden prices, and selections that do not affect the configured chair.

## Design Principles

- Keep configuration choices easy to scan and compare.
- Reflect every supported choice in the 3D preview.
- Keep pricing visible and explainable.
- Use accessible keyboard controls and respect reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard operation, expose selected states to assistive technology, label color options by name, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
